## Goal

Resolve the capacity bottleneck observed in B14 by aggressively scaling the number of affine coupling stages ($S = 2 \log_2(D) - 2$) and widening the subnetworks (`hidden_units = 256`) for the upscaled 512D configuration, while utilizing a moderately complex 2-layer random MLP manifold generator (`mlp_depth = 2`).

## Hypotheses

### H15.1: Aggressive Logarithmic Depth Scaling
An aggressive logarithmic stage scaling ($S = 2 \log_2(D) - 2$) provides sufficient permutation and mixing capability to untangle the manifold generated by a 2-layer random MLP, avoiding representation collapse or incomplete alignment across all test dimensions $D \in \{32, 64, 128, 256, 512\}$.

### H15.2: Hidden Capacity Width for 512D
Increasing subnetwork capacity (`hidden_units = 256`) at the extremely high ambient space of 512D resolves the optimization bottleneck and permits clean noise-dimension suppression, achieving a diagonality ratio $\ge 0.85$ and a Found Common Rank of exactly 3 on the 512D dataset.

### H15.3: Dimensionality-Independent 3D Visualization
The robust non-string-based visualizer logic (based on `shape[1] >= 3` check) will successfully render 3D diagnostic coordinates for all sweep dimensions ($32 \le D \le 512$) on W&B, demonstrating consistent alignment of the 3 shared latent coordinates.
